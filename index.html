Mechanics had a reputation. I named my shop New Image because I wanted to change it, one honest job at a time.

Amir Owner, New Image Auto Services

Amir opened New Image on Talbert Avenue decades ago. The whole idea is in the name: most people walk into a repair shop braced to be overcharged, so he set out to be the shop where that never happens.

He tells you what your car actually needs and what it costs. When a repair can wait, he says so. When a big job can be split up, he helps you spread it out. The customers who found him in the nineties are still bringing their cars, and now their kids’ cars too.
